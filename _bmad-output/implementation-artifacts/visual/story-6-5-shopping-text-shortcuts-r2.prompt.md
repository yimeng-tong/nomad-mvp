## Prompt

Create a high-fidelity Chinese mobile travel-app UX board with three complete portrait phones,
side by side on a white landscape canvas. Title outside phones: "Story 6.5 | 轻量购物记录".
Use the reference's compact white mobile screens, restrained dark-green accents, neutral dividers,
black Chinese typography and simple familiar icons. All three phone frames and bottom safe areas
must fit fully within the image. No brand logo, marketing decoration, nested cards or gradients.

Panel A exterior label "A 直接写想买的". Header "购物记录" with back arrow. The main surface is
ONE generous multiline text input. Its empty grey placeholder is exactly:
"想买什么？可以直接描述
款式：
数量：
预算：".
Inside the lower edge of this same composer is a compact shortcut row with text buttons
"款式" "数量" "预算" "品牌" "尺码". No separate attribute fields, no store section, no date
selector. Bottom "保存记录" command is disabled grey while input is empty. Exterior caption:
"占位只是示例，补充项都可留空".

Panel B exterior label "B 点击标签继续写". Same screen and same single input, now containing
real black user text exactly:
"鼓浪屿冰箱贴
款式：日光岩款
数量：2
预算：".
Place an insertion caret just after the final colon. The 预算 shortcut in the same lower input
row has a subtle active indication. Green command "保存记录" remains enabled because the actual
shopping description exists even with an empty budget. Do not display a full keyboard; retain
ample bottom safe area and focus indication. Exterior caption: "自动换行插入，保留已经写的内容".

Panel C exterior label "C 保存为一条记录". Header "行程清单" with back arrow. A plain section
"必买目标" with one unchecked record. Its exact multiline text is:
"鼓浪屿冰箱贴
款式：日光岩款
数量：2
预算：150元".
Use readable line spacing and a small edit-pencil icon. Small factual status "已保存". Bottom
only "+ 添加记录". No auto-scheduled visit, linked shops, purchase date, typed-attribute table,
store images or schedule undo countdown. Exterior caption: "只保存购物想法，不自动安排".

The placeholders are grey and the typed content is black. Label shortcuts are optional text
insertion actions, not required choice chips, radio buttons or tabs. Every label/action is large
enough for a mobile tap. Preserve clear mobile proportions and text wrapping. Explanatory captions
belong only outside the phones. No watermark, reservation/stock/verified badges, fake location,
background tracking, price guarantees, structured form rows or unwanted additional controls.
