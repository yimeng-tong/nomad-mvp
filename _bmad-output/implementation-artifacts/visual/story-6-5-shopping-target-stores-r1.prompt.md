# Story 6.5 Shopping Target and Stores

Status: proposed for Story 6.5 review.
Tool: built-in ImageGen; new board in the established checklist visual style.

## Prompt

Create a high-fidelity Chinese mobile travel-app board with three complete portrait phones side
by side on white. Board title outside phones: "Story 6.5 | 必买目标与可选门店". Clean white
mobile UI, black typography, restrained deep-green accents, neutral dividers, compact fields,
recognizable outline icons. No branded header, version labels, decorative gradients or nested cards.
Use realistic shop/landmark thumbnail images. All phone frames and home indicators fully visible.

A exterior title "A 先记下想买的". Full page opened from 行程清单, back icon. Page title
"鼓浪屿冰箱贴". Item status checkbox "未完成". Compact editable rows: "款式  日光岩款",
"数量  2", "预算  150元", "日期  整趟". Section title "可选门店", neutral empty text
"还没有关联门店" and plus command "关联门店". No required address field or blocking error.
Exterior caption "目标可以先存在，不要求选店".

B exterior title "B 选择可选门店". Same background with bottom Sheet titled "关联门店".
Compact city selector "厦门" and search field "搜索门店名称". Two example canonical store rows
with small shop photos and right-hand selection checkboxes: "厦门礼物·龙头路店" with address
fragment "鼓浪屿 · 龙头路", and "厦门礼物·中山路店" with "中山路商圈". Both selected in example.
Small factual text "库存未确认". Green button "关联这 2 家". Source rows/addresses are illustrative
fixtures, not actual stock or verification promises. Exterior caption "关联地点，不自动加入行程".

C exterior title "C 一个目标，多家选择". Full target page with the same title/attributes and
unchecked 未完成 status. 可选门店 section shows the two associated stores as plain rows with
thumbnails, address fragment, chevron and overflow menu. A small plus command "关联更多门店".
Tapping the place opens the reusable POI information Sheet; there is no scheduled time next to
these stores. Exterior caption "查看地点后，可沿现有流程加入计划". Do not show radio buttons here
or imply all stores must be visited. Do not auto-check the target when associating a store.

Keep ordinary stores visually consistent with existing POI rows. No stock count, booking claim,
shopping checklist source legend, live tracking, 48-hour reminder, map or schedule undo control.
All explanatory captions stay outside phones. Use at least visibly 44pt touch targets; text must
fit and wrap cleanly. No watermark or external decorations.
